crazy

/ˈkreɪzi/
adj.疯狂的；荒唐的；着迷的；极热衷的

近义词

mad insane wild absurd nuts eccentric

例句

  • You must be crazy to go out in this storm!
    你在这场暴风雨中出门一定是疯了！
  • She is crazy about jazz music and collects old records.
    她极其热衷于爵士乐，收藏了很多老唱片。
  • It sounds crazy, but the plan actually worked.
    听起来很荒唐，但这个计划居然奏效了。
